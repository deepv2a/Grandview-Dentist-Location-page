Dental Bridges

Dental bridges replace missing teeth with a fixed restoration, meaning it stays in place rather than coming out like a denture, that can help with chewing, speaking, and the appearance of your smile.

Root Canal Treatment

Dental Insurance and CDCP Information

Our copy

Sunrise Village Dental accepts most dental plans registered in British Columbia and most plans from across Canada. Coverage varies depending on your policy, the treatment recommended, and your plan limits.

We also accept the Canadian Dental Care Plan (CDCP) and align our rates with the CDCP Federal Fee Guide. Patients with 100% CDCP coverage may pay nothing out of pocket for eligible treatments, while others may have a co-payment.

You're welcome to bring your insurance or CDCP details when you visit. Our team can help you understand your coverage and discuss expected costs before treatment begins.
